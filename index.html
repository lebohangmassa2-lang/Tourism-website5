Lesotho, the Kingdom in the Sky

Every metre of this country sits above 1,000 m. Come for the waterfalls, rock art, pony trails and winter snow.

Snow season: Afriski usually opens from June to August. Pack warm layers and check road conditions on Sani Pass.

About Lesotho

Lesotho is a small mountain kingdom completely surrounded by South Africa. Its lowest point is 1,400 m above sea level, which is why people call it the Kingdom in the Sky.

The Basotho people are known for the woollen blanket worn across the shoulders, the straw hat called the mokorotlo, and a strong tradition of horse riding. Villages are often reached on foot or by pony, so a visit here is slow and personal.

What you can do here

  • Hike to waterfalls and high mountain passes
  • See San rock art and the cave dwellings at Ha Kome
  • Ski in winter and trek by pony in summer
  • Stay in village lodges and hear local choirs

Before you go

Many nationalities can enter visa-free for short stays. Check the rules for your passport with the Lesotho embassy before you travel.

The currency is the Loti (LSL), pegged one to one with the South African rand, which most places also accept. Carry cash for remote villages.

Summer (Oct - Mar) is green and warm with waterfalls at full flow. Winter (Jun - Aug) brings snow and skiing. Nights are cold all year.

Most visitors drive or fly into Maseru via Johannesburg or Bloemfontein. Mountain roads need care, and a 4x4 is best for Sani Pass and remote parks.
