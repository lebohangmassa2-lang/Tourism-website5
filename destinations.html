Destinations

17 places across the highlands. Search, filter and save the ones you like.

Adventure

Afriski Mountain Resort

Butha-Buthe

Southern Africa's ski resort at roughly 3,050 m. Ski and snowboard in winter, mountain bike and hike in summer.

Nature

Bokong Nature Reserve

Leribe

A high-altitude reserve near the Mafika-Lisiu Pass (about 3,090 m) with the Lepaqoa waterfall and a visitor centre.

Heritage

Ha Baroana Rock Art

Maseru

One of the finest San painting sites in Lesotho, showing eland and human figures on a sheltered rock face.

Culture

Ha Kome Cave Dwellings

Berea

Families still live in houses built into sandstone overhangs, a tradition that goes back to the 1800s.

Nature

Katse Dam

Thaba-Tseka

A 185 m arch dam in the Lesotho Highlands Water Project. Take the dam wall tour, then fish or boat on the lake.

Heritage

Liphofung Cave

Butha-Buthe

A sandstone shelter with San rock art that King Moshoeshoe I once used. The cultural site has a small museum.

Culture

Malealea

Mafeteng

A village lodge and the starting point for pony treks through the Gates of Paradise Pass. Evenings feature the village choir.

Nature

Maletsunyane Falls

Semonkong, Maseru

A 192 m single-drop waterfall in the Thaba-Putsoa range. Home of the world's longest commercial single-drop abseil.

Culture

Maseru and the Basotho Hat

Maseru

The capital city: craft markets, the Basotho Hat building, restaurants and the gateway to every route in the country.

Nature

Mohale Dam

Central Highlands

A rockfill dam in a wide highland valley. Quiet viewpoints, fishing and sweeping mountain scenery.

Culture

Morija Museum and Archives

Maseru

The oldest mission station in Lesotho (1833), with a museum, dinosaur footprints and the annual Morija Arts and Cultural Festival.

Nature

Qiloane Falls

Thaba-Bosiu area, Maseru

A waterfall beside Qiloane hill, whose shape inspired the Basotho hat (mokorotlo). A short drive from Thaba-Bosiu.

Adventure

Sani Pass

Mokhotlong

The highest road pass in southern Africa, a steep 4x4 route to the border with South Africa at about 2,876 m.

Nature

Sehlabathebe National Park

Qacha's Nek

Remote sandstone arches and rock pools, part of the Maloti-Drakensberg World Heritage Site. Good for trout fishing and quiet hiking.

Heritage

Thaba-Bosiu

Maseru

The flat-topped mountain fortress where King Moshoeshoe I founded the Basotho nation in 1824. Guided walks cover his grave and the old fortifications.

Adventure

Thabana Ntlenyana

Mokhotlong

At 3,482 m, the highest peak in southern Africa. A challenging multi-day hike or pony trek from Sani Top.

Nature

Ts'ehlanyane National Park

Butha-Buthe

Indigenous forest, alpine meadows and bearded vultures. Hike, ride horses or camp beside clear mountain streams.
