Budget tip

Camps and village lodges cost under M600. Add pony trekking and meals for a full mountain experience at a low price.

Booking tips

Afriski and the lodges near Sani Pass fill up from June to August. Reserve at least a month ahead.

Nights are cold all year. Confirm that rooms have heaters or fireplaces, and bring a warm sleeping bag for camps.

Small lodges prefer cash in Maloti or rand. Card machines can fail in remote areas.
